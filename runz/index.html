RUNZ

Your magazine is the build. A top-down roguelite arena shooter where every bullet you load changes the run.

Play the Demo on Steam itch.iO Download Kit Discord

Assemble the perfect magazine.

Survive twenty rounds of escalating waves, then a boss. Between rounds, spend your cash in the shop: buy bullets, merge three copies into a stronger one, enchant slots, equip mods, and choose the order your magazine fires in. Then let the build loose.

  • 90 bullets, 23 synergies and 89 mods to draft from
  • Merge 3 copies of a bullet to star it up and pick its upgrade
  • Auto-battler style synergies that read your whole magazine
  • Enchant slots so any bullet inherits a new synergy
  • 7 characters, each with its own magazine and rule
  • 5 Depth difficulty tiers, each ending on its own boss, plus an endless mode
  • 43 achievements that unlock characters, bullets, mods and deeper Depths
